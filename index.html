FAQ

Questions researchers usually ask.

Do you guarantee journal acceptance?

No. AA Research Publication can improve manuscript quality, journal fit, submission readiness, and reviewer responses, but editorial decisions remain entirely with the journal and its reviewers.

Can you help select a Scopus or Web of Science journal?

Yes. Journal recommendations can consider disciplinary scope, article type, quartile, indexing status, audience, publication model, and the manuscript's current readiness.

Do your prices include journal publication fees or APCs?

No. The listed prices cover AA Research Publication's professional services. Publisher APCs, open-access charges, or other journal fees are separate and are paid directly according to the publisher's policy.

Can you work with reviewer comments after a major or minor revision?

Yes. Reviewer-response support can include an itemised response letter, manuscript revisions, change highlighting, consistency checking, and resubmission preparation.

How is confidentiality handled?

Manuscripts, datasets, reviewer reports, and personal information should be treated as confidential project materials. A formal confidentiality or NDA option can also be offered for sensitive projects.

How long does a project take?

Turnaround depends on manuscript length and scope. A focused edit may require a few working days, while substantive development, analysis, or major reviewer revisions require a longer project plan agreed before work begins.
